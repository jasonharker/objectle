# Primary Material is the largest visible surface

Many objects are made of several materials, and the Primary Material Attribute has to name one of them. We decided it is the material covering the largest visible surface of a typical example of the noun. This means a hatchet is wood and an armchair is fabric, even where people would say "a hatchet is metal". Feedback is only fair if a player can check it, and the visible surface is the one thing every player sees: in the stage-5 render, and in every Ghost.

## Considered Options

- **What most people would say it's made of** — rejected: intuitive, but disputed cases (armchair, sneaker) need a tiebreaker, and players cannot verify it from the model.
- **The material of the part that does the job** — rejected: gets arbitrary fast (umbrella canopy, racket strings).

## Consequences

- Some Feedback will surprise players. Objects where the rule fights intuition (hatchet, hammer) stay in the Guess Vocabulary but are kept out of the Answer Pool.
- Every noun's Primary Material has to be judged against a typical example, not against the specific sourced model.
