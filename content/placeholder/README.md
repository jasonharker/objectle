# Placeholder content bundle

What the deployed walking skeleton serves until the curation build ships the
real content bundle from the private content repo (ADR-0004). It is the test
fixture's vocabulary and anonymized stage-1 placeholders, with a Schedule that
alternates them for Puzzle Numbers 1-365. Each object's six Reveal Stages all
reuse its stage-1 placeholder, so no stage discloses more than stage 1. Each
noun's model, drawn as its Ghost, is a plain box whose longest dimension is its
Size. Nothing here is real curation.
